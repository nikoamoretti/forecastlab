Synthetic pre-cutoff labor demand indicators note

SYNTHETIC WORKFLOW EVIDENCE — NOT A REAL SOURCE DOCUMENT.

This static validation fixture summarizes only labor-demand signals available by 2 April 2024. It is not a copied release and must not be used as real forecasting evidence.

Job openings and hiring measures had eased from unusually strong earlier levels, but the available indicators still described a labor market with substantial demand. Initial unemployment claims had not shown the kind of sustained surge that commonly accompanies a rapid rise in the unemployment rate.

The signals can affect the forecast in opposite directions. Cooling vacancies support a gradual softening scenario, while stable claims weaken the case for an abrupt deterioration. A node-level forecast should record both the direction of the indicators and the uncertainty in their lead time.
